## Multiple Sequence Alignment Analysis

Clustal Omega alignment of human SMARCA4 and yeast SNF2 revealed a highly conserved region in the central portion of the protein (~positions 750–1200). This region corresponds to the ATP-dependent helicase catalytic core.

Long stretches of exact matches (*) and chemically similar substitutions (:) indicate strong evolutionary conservation. Minimal gaps were observed in this region, suggesting structural constraint.

In contrast, N-terminal and C-terminal regions showed lower conservation and increased divergence.
